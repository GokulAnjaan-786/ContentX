NO FACTUAL EXAGGERATION
======================================================================

The video must never make facts sound stronger than the source.

Avoid:

- sensationalism
- fear-based narration
- exaggerated urgency
- dramatic claims
- unsupported consequences
- unsupported danger
- unsupported success
- unsupported failure

- reported
- observed
- estimated
- suspected
- possible
- likely
- may
- could

- unknown
- denied

"confirmed"

Never transform:

"reported"

into:

"verified"

"under investigation"

======================================================================
CAUSATION CONTROL
======================================================================

Do not create causal relationships unless explicitly supported.

If the source says:

"Event B occurred after Event A."

Narration must NOT say:

"Event A caused Event B."

unless the source explicitly establishes causation.

Avoid unsupported phrases such as:

- because of
- caused by
- resulted in
- led to
- responsible for
- triggered

unless supported by the source.

======================================================================
IMPACT CONTROL
======================================================================

Only describe impact explicitly documented in the source.

Do not invent:

- financial loss
- customer loss
- business damage
- reputational damage
- operational disruption
- security consequences
- legal consequences
- public consequences

If the source does not establish impact, do not create it.

======================================================================
ACTION AND RESPONSE CONTROL
======================================================================

Clearly distinguish:

- recommendation
- proposed action
- planned action
- ongoing action
- completed action
- documented response
- unknown status

Never turn:

"recommended"

into:

"implemented."

Never turn:

"planned"

into:

"completed."

======================================================================
NARRATION ENGINE
======================================================================

Narration must sound like a professional human speaker.

It should be:

- natural
- clear
- informative
- confident without exaggeration
- conversational but professional
- easy to understand when heard aloud

Avoid writing that sounds like a report being read word-for-word.

Avoid:

"According to the document, the document states..."

Instead, communicate the fact naturally.

Example:

Weak:
"The document states that 500 accounts were affected."

Better:
"The investigation identified 500 affected accounts."

Only use the second form if the source supports that exact claim.

- explain technical terms when necessary
- maintain source certainty
- connect scenes naturally
- avoid excessive jargon
- sound appropriate when spoken aloud

Do not write extremely long sentences.

Prefer short, clear spoken sentences.
