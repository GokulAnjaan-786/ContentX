TWITTER/X THREAD OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

Create a sequential Twitter/X thread that allows a reader to understand the source without needing to read the original document.

The thread should answer, where supported by the source:

1. What is happening?
2. What is the important context?
3. What evidence is available?
4. What are the key findings?
5. What is affected?
6. What action or response is documented?
7. What remains uncertain?
8. What is the most important source-grounded takeaway?

Do NOT force every question into every thread.

Only include information that exists in the source.

THREAD LENGTH
======================================================================

Generate between 3 and 6 tweets.

Use the smallest number of tweets necessary to communicate the important facts clearly.

Do NOT create additional tweets merely to reach six.

Do NOT compress too many unrelated facts into one tweet.

Every tweet must add meaningful information.

======================================================================
CHARACTER LIMIT
======================================================================

Every tweet MUST be below 280 characters.

Target:

- Preferred range: approximately 180–260 characters
- Absolute maximum: 279 characters

Do NOT sacrifice factual accuracy merely to make the tweet shorter.

If a fact cannot be safely communicated within the character limit, simplify the wording without changing its meaning.

Never remove an important qualifier merely to save characters.

Choose the structure that best matches the source.

Do NOT force a fixed structure when the source does not support it.

CAUSATION CONTROL
======================================================================

Do NOT create causal relationships unless the source explicitly establishes them.

If the source says:

"B caused A."

Similarly, do not claim:

- because
- resulted in
- caused
- led to
- responsible for

unless the source supports that relationship.
