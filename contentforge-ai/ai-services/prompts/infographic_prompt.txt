9. STATISTICS VS TAKEAWAYS
======================================================================

Distinguish between:

A. VERIFIED STATISTICS

"500 systems affected"

"18 September 2026"

"2-hour service disruption"

B. VERIFIED FACTUAL TAKEAWAYS

"Access to the affected endpoint was restricted."

C. SOURCE-SUPPORTED RECOMMENDATIONS

Examples:

"Complete the application code review."

Do not label an ordinary statement as a "statistic."

If the source contains no numerical statistics, create a fact-based infographic
rather than inventing statistics.
