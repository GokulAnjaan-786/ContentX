======================================================================
ATOMIC FACT EXTRACTION
======================================================================

Extract EVERY meaningful atomic factual assertion.

An atomic fact is one independently verifiable statement.

Example:

Source:
"Northstar Systems identified unauthorized access affecting 500 accounts on September 18."

Do NOT store this as one large mixed fact if it contains multiple independently meaningful claims.

Prefer:

Fact 1:
"Northstar Systems identified unauthorized access."

Fact 2:
"500 accounts were affected."

Fact 3:
"The event was identified on September 18."

However, preserve relationships between these facts.

Do NOT split a statement when doing so destroys its meaning.

======================================================================
FACT BOUNDARY RULE
======================================================================

A fact should be split when it contains multiple independently meaningful assertions.

A fact should remain together when:

- the relationship is essential to meaning
- separating it would create ambiguity
- the qualification belongs directly to the assertion
- the certainty applies to the complete statement

Example:

"Approximately 500 systems may have been affected."

Do NOT create:

"500 systems were affected."

Instead preserve:

"Approximately 500 systems may have been affected."

The uncertainty and approximation are part of the fact.

======================================================================
NO INFERENCE RULE
======================================================================

Do not infer information that the source does not explicitly establish.

Examples:

Source:
"Access occurred after credentials were exposed."

Do NOT automatically infer:

"Credential exposure caused the attack."

Source:
"System A was unavailable for 3 hours."

Do NOT infer:

"Customers lost revenue."

Source:
"The report discusses a vulnerability."

Do NOT infer:

"The vulnerability was exploited."

Source:
"The company announced an investigation."

Do NOT infer:

"The company was responsible."

======================================================================
CAUSALITY RULE
======================================================================

Distinguish carefully between:

- correlation
- sequence
- association
- attribution
- causation

Only record causation when the source explicitly establishes it.

If the source says:

"After X, Y occurred."

Record the sequence.

Do not rewrite it as:

"X caused Y."
